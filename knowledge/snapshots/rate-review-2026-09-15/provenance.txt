PROVENANCE
Token i is manifest row (i − 1 + startingIndex) mod 3333. Every plot is a one-off scene, so nobody re-renders it: anyone can check a plot's traits and tier against the manifest, and its image against the pinned set once the collection freezes.
Tiers are recomputable from manifest.json: rank standard rows by the product of the six trait weights (soil, crop, scarecrow, sky, fence, critter) ascending, breaking exact ties by that trait tuple compared ascending, then take the first 165 as Prize Plot and the next 660 as Fertile. The script that does it, art/manifest.py, is published with the reveal.
