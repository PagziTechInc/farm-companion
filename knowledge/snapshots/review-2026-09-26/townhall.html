THE MAYOR'S DRAWINGS

The rules, plainly:

TicketsBought with $CROP and burned on the spot, every one. The barrel is a furnace.
Free entryEvery planted plot gets one entry each week, automatically. Farming is a ticket.
The prizeThe Mayor buys a plot from the open market and raffles it, with cosmetics from the wardrobe alongside. His budget is capped by what that week's tickets burned, so the math always holds. A thin week rolls the barrel into the next one.
The drawEvery Monday at the weather boundary: a sealed seed committed before entries open is revealed and picks the winner. Same machinery that seals the weather. Anyone can verify it.

Full rules and the ticket window are published before the first drawing.
