01GETTING HERE

Robinhood Chain runs on ETH. Three steps and a couple of minutes gets you farming.

2Add the network. One click below adds Robinhood Chain to your wallet.
3Move ETH over. OpenSea's swap moves ETH from Ethereum to Robinhood Chain in one step, free of fees, in a minute or two, and runs both ways.

02THE LOOP

1Mint a plot. 3,333 exist. Your farm's art, crop, soil and rarity are revealed shortly after the mint closes.
2Plant it. Wake the plot with 2,500 $CROP, or take a seed bag from Mabel in one click: 0.001 ETH at the opening, then repriced every day to what 2,500 $CROP costs in the pool. From that second it earns its rate.
3Harvest. Whenever you like. One click collects one plot; Harvest all collects every plot in a single transaction.
4Upgrade. Burn $CROP to climb five levels, LV 1 to LV 5. Each level is permanent and raises the plot's weight.

03HOW PLOTS
ARE DEALT

Every farm was drawn before the mint and laid out in a table of 3,333 rows. The table's hash is on-chain already. What nobody knows, us included, is which token lands on which row.

token id−1+starting indexmod3,333=manifest row

The starting index comes from a block hash that did not exist when the table was built. Because it is one offset applied to every token, each row is used exactly once: no two plots can come out the same and none are left over.

Rarity is part of the row, so it is dealt the same way: 33 Golden Acres, 165 Prize Plots, 660 Fertile, 2,475 Common, in a proportion fixed before anyone paid. After reveal the tiers freeze and no key, ours included, can edit them. The Provenance page compares the table's hash against the chain, live.

04WEIGHT:
WHO EARNS WHAT

Every planted plot earns up to 2,000 $CROP a week per weight unit, times the weather. Weight = level times rarity. Dormant plots earn nothing. Your rate is yours: nobody planting after you lowers it.

your $CROP / week=2,000×First Soil×weather×your weight

First Soil is 2× in the Founding Week, Sep 21 to 28, then 1.5× until Oct 19, then 1, for every planted plot: the earlier you plant, the more of it you get. The ceiling applies to the rate before First Soil and the weather: the farm's base rate reaches the year's weekly budget at about 3,644 weight units, about the whole collection planted plus a few upgrades, and above that the base is shared by weight. First Soil and sunny weeks are paid from the Granary on top, while it lasts.

Each week is committed in advance and sealed on chain, then revealed at the boundary: nobody, including us, can change a week after it is sealed. Sunny weeks pay above the base rate with the surplus drawn from the Granary; harsh weeks pay under it and the difference flows back in. Some weeks bring events: Flash Floods (2×, 24h) and Harvest Moons (2×, 48h) stack on the week, capped at 2× in total. The Town Hall shows the live week and the sealed ones ahead.

08WHAT TRAVELS
WITH A PLOT

Sell a plot and its level, its rarity and its unharvested $CROP all go with it. Planting does not: a traded plot arrives dormant and the new farmer plants it fresh.

Levels are burned into the plot and can never be taken away. Unharvested $CROP belongs to the plot too, so a farm with a standing harvest is worth more than an idle one. Planting clears on transfer by design: yield follows the farmer who planted, not a bot passing tokens around.

Selling? Harvest before you list. Whatever is still standing on the day the deed changes hands goes to the buyer, and the County shows it on every listed plot so nobody is surprised.

10THE MAYOR'S DRAWINGS

From October: every Monday, right when the new weather breaks, the Mayor pulls a winner from the ticket barrel. Every ticket is burned. Every planted plot enters once, free.

The prize is a plot bought from the open market with a budget capped by what that week's tickets burned, so the drawing can never spend what it did not take in. The draw uses the same sealed-seed machinery as the weather, so anyone can verify the winner. Full rules hang at the Town Hall.
