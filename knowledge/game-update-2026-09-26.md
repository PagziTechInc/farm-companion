# September 26, 2026 game update

The Almanac remains gameplay authority. Public pages and client files are saved with SHA-256 hashes in [index.json](snapshots/review-2026-09-26/index.json). [Pinned chain evidence](snapshots/review-2026-09-26/chain-review.json) is from chain 4663, block 72,697,223, September 26 at 01:37:29 UTC. No wallet transaction or signature was sent.

## What changed

- NativeActivationV3 is `0x75bb7544c07353f500e531bc89f9518b4d2bd32a`. Both emissions.activation and the NFT transfer hook point there. Its exact-match Sourcify deployed bytecode matches independent RPC bytes. Its immutable previous registry is V2 at `0xd87fb27b292e48fafbafb860ba24583acb50404b`; its source-runtime hash also matches the pinned observation. [V3 source](snapshots/review-2026-09-26/NativeActivationV3.sol) and [V2 source](snapshots/review-2026-09-26/NativeActivationV2.sol) were reviewed for planting, transfer and read-through behavior. All five other core runtimes remain unchanged.
- V3 reads untouched activation records through V2; a local record overrides the old book after planting or clearing. Each planting checks both links and plantingOpen. The previous registry rejects new plantings once unwired. Transfers clear activation and attempt emissions synchronization; caught sync failures remain material.
- Seed bags are fixed at **0.001 ETH**, capped at **3,333 across V2/V3**, and only for a plot's first planting by either payment route. Transfers do not restore eligibility. `bagAvailable` also checks treasury funding. There were 864 funded bags available at the review block; this is historical, not inventory promised to a visitor.
- Sprouts are an ETH replanting route for dormant plots planted before, after Genesis. `sproutsAvailable` checks timing, links, switches and treasury burn capacity. `sproutPrice` was 0.001334 ETH at the review block; refresh it. Owner repricing is bounded only by the 0.001 ETH floor; the keeper has a configurable price band, step and cooldown. The contract accepts at least the price and refunds excess. The companion reviews an exact quote and invalidates a draft on repricing. Bags and sprouts each burn 1,500 treasury CROP.
- The collection is sold out: supply 3,333, offset 1,827 and tiersFinalized true at the pinned block. Current ownership and balances still require a fresh read.
- The oracle has unchanged code but its owner is now the published Weather Seal (`0x060543f9a55dbdf12f4d2b98a154a1d099559f7a`). Almanac promises sealed weeks and floods. The seal was unavailable from the attempted Sourcify/Blockscout API reads; ownership alone does not prove every keeper/admin path enforces commitments. The old direct-owner replacement finding must not be presented as the current control structure.
- Orchard is a separately funded weekly AAPL-stock-token programme. The published first pot is 2 AAPL for September 28–October 5 UTC. Shares use actual earning-week production credited to each wallet, not current token balance, old pending harvest or today's weight. Claims need verified allocations after the week closes and ETH gas, with no CROP approval. Future baskets are not guaranteed. The companion links to the official records and excludes unverified AAPL from profit totals.

## Unchanged

Nominal yield remains 2,000 CROP/week × own active weight × weather × First Soil, with annual base/carry and finite Granary limits. Upgrade burns remain 5k/10k/20k/50k. A rising valley weight affects binding funding limits, not an uncapped personal rate. First Soil becomes 1.5× September 28 and ends October 19 at 00:00 UTC.

The official client now suggests 100,000-CROP allowances. The companion deliberately retains exact per-action allowances and separate human confirmations. Prior broadcasts against the old registry remain recoverable; that legacy target cannot receive new companion drafts. Orchard claims and batch plantings are handled by the official site, not inferred from import data.
